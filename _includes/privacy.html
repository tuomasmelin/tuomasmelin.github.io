Privacy

This site counts page views with PostHog, hosted in the EU. It's set up to be as anonymous as possible: nothing is stored on your device (no cookies), no visitor profiles are created, and clicks and sessions aren't recorded.

For each page view PostHog receives the page address, the referring site, your browser and device type, and an approximate location based on your IP address.

The data is only used to see which pages people read. It isn't used for advertising, sold or shared.
